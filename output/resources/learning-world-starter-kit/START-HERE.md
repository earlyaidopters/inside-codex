# Build your first learning world

By Mark Kashef. Companion to You Can Now Build 3D Worlds to Learn Anything.

Start with one subject, one room, and one lesson. GAME-PRODUCTION-PLAN.md is the unchanged original plan behind Inside Codex. Its full scope is much larger than a first experiment, and its performance figures are proposed targets, not a promise for your computer or account. Give your agent the plan as a structural reference and ask it to reduce the scope to fit you.

1. Install Blender from https://www.blender.org/download/ and open your coding agent.
2. In Codex, search the Plugins catalog for Blender. Read the current cards and complete their setup. Installed tools and available cards can change. Game Development Studio and Build 3D Game Rooms were present in Mark's environment; they are not a claim that everybody sees the same catalog results.
3. Give the agent BLENDER-AND-HOSTING.md and STARTING-PROMPT.txt. Replace the topic and learner fields before sending.
4. Review the plan, then build and check one complete lesson before expanding.
5. Check the result yourself and ask someone in the target audience to try it.
6. Publish the production output and verify its live link.

No specific subscription guarantees a completion time or percentage of usage. Check your current account allowance and keep the first scope small. The complete Inside Codex build took roughly eighteen hours, including testing and refinement.

The game webpage contains practice simulations. It does not connect visitors' actual Codex accounts. Changing the subject requires reviewing the learning content and interactions, not just replacing names.
