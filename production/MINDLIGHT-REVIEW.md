## Smallest empirical check

The largest uncertainty is planning-chapter momentum. Show comparable qualified viewers one of two private cuts with the **same title, thumbnail, narration and section order**: planning slides alone, or those slides with the two genuine game-consequence cutaways described above. Ask what each planning decision changed, where they would skip, and what first interaction they would build. Do not ask whether they liked it first.

The pacing hypothesis is weakened if the cutaways do not improve understood consequences or reduce the same reported skip points; the remaining cause may be narration density or audience qualification. A simple blind package-comprehension check should follow when one thumbnail is actually rendered, without generating a variant batch.
