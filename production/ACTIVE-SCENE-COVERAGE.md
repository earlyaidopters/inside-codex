# Active-scene coverage and compact-screen atlas access

The previous 90-second warm traversal measured fresh mission introductions and moving camera transitions. It did not by itself cover the wide arrival scene, successful interactions, flow-specific failures/repairs, or the completed-tour return. The new active-scenes scenario runs the complete visible-control journey and collects ten rendered-frame inventories at every named capture point. It adds an explicit successful-result capture before each step advances, retains flow-specific evidence, and includes the independent capstone and resource/save checks.

These are point samples after the camera settles. They do not replace the moving traversal, continuous-animation maxima, final repeat study or audio-enabled soak. Read-only inventory and screenshots perturb timing, so this scenario deliberately reports submission counts and triangles without frame-time claims. Build files and test sources are hashed before and after, and browser/graphics/quality/render-size metadata is recorded.

## Candidate to evaluate next

A bounded candidate can group compatible geometry within each exhibit: permission gate frames/ink/retained cards; review desk frames and supports; steering rails and selector stems; observatory supports and stationary route/status backplates; context rack rails; branch stem/rail; and the harness’s four fixed circuit tubes, whose material switches together. Keep doors, selectable port rings, moving file/model mounts, status lights, scanner/pulse meshes and control faces separate. Do not flatten materials across different finishes or alter text textures. The grouping must remain owned by each factory and preserve root enable/disposal behavior.

This is a hypothesis, not a measured improvement. Before editing, create a named goal from the verified High baseline with an explicit source allowlist, target and one-candidate limit. Validate geometry/materials, controls, quality changes, context recovery, residency eviction/reconstruction, matched fixed views and active outcomes before a candidate capture. Assess Balanced and the moving traversal separately. Do not reuse a candidate run or silently extend the goal if it misses.
