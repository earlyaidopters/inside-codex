# Optional community learning pilot

This is a prepared research outline, not a completed study. No invitations or messages have been sent.

Ask Mark and five to eight representative community members to try the experience on their normal equipment. Record browser/device and graphics setting. Explain that the exercises are authored simulations. Avoid coaching the answers and do not provide the capstone walkthrough.

Before playing, ask each participant to outline how they would repair an onboarding record with conflicting old/current sources, a missing approval field and a saved-output check. Score the same five criteria before and after: task definition, current sources, appropriate access/tools, verification of actual output, and a reusable tested procedure. Use an equivalent second fixture, not an identical answer-memory test.

Observe whether the participant can enter, complete the context, verification and reusable-procedure missions, and attempt the Cedar capstone without a worked solution. Record unaided completion, requests for help, hints, first submission, time, abandonments and the exact confusing control or wording. Ask the participant to explain why raising effort would not repair a missing source connection and why a saved result needs a reload/check.

Use a short feedback record:

- Participant alias and experience level; device/browser and graphics setting.
- Started without coaching? Core route completed unaided? Capstone first result and hints?
- Exact point of confusion, failure or discomfort; screenshot and reproduction if available.
- One habit they can now perform in their own Codex workspace.
- Pre/post rubric scores and one observed task-transfer example.
- Whether they would return or recommend it, and why.

The plan's exploratory targets are 80% unaided core completion and a median improvement of 20 percentage points. Report raw counts and individual results for this small sample. Do not imply statistical certainty, certification, economic return or broad learning validation from assistant playthroughs.
